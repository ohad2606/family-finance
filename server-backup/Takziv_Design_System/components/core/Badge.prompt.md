Small pill for status and labels. The soft tints are the financial-health chips; the solid fills mark transaction kind.

```jsx
<Badge tone="excellent">מעולה</Badge>
<Badge tone="fair">בינוני</Badge>
<Badge tone="income">הכנסה</Badge>
```

Tones: `excellent | good | fair | poor | na` (soft fg-on-tint) and `brass | income | expense` (solid, white text).
